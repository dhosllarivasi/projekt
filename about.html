A river worth protecting

The Vjosa is a living landscape—wild, beautiful and rich in life.

The Vjosa

One of Europe's last wild rivers

Flowing freely from the mountains to the Adriatic Sea, the Vjosa and its tributaries create a rare network of natural habitats.

Visitors can find quiet villages, rich wildlife and outdoor experiences shaped by the river. Our goal is to share this extraordinary place responsibly and support sustainable local tourism.
